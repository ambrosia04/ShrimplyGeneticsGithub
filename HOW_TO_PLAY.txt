======================================================================
                  SHRIMPLY GENETICS - PLAYER'S GUIDE
======================================================================

1. GETTING STARTED
------------------
- Open "index.html" in your web browser.
- Click "Play" on the title screen.
- You begin with $100. Open the "Shop" tab (Hotkey: S) and buy your first
  pair of shrimp (e.g., up to 4 Red Cherry).
- It has a system so 1 every 3 consecutive genders is the other!

1.5. SPOILER WARNING
------------------
- If you want to learn as you play, stop reading here and explore!
- If you like knowing what does what, keep reading to your heart's content!

2. BREEDING & SADDLES
---------------------
- Mature female shrimp develop a "Saddle" on their back (unfertilized eggs).
- Breeding checks occur automatically every minute (or faster at higher speeds).
- If a compatible adult male is in the tank, the female will become "Berried" 
  (pregnant) and carry eggs underneath her tail.
- Once the pregnancy timer completes, an exclamation icon (!) will appear
  above her. Click her to open the Culling / Birth menu.


3. CULLING & GENETICS
---------------------
- Each baby inherits one allele from the mother and one from the father.
- Dominant colors display physically, but recessive alleles are hidden.
- In the Culling menu, inspect your baby shrimp:
    * "NEW" badges indicate an undiscovered species or allele.
    * Use the dropdowns to filter by specific allele, species, or gender.
    * Use "Keep" to move them into your tank or "Sell" to earn cash.
    * Keyboard shortcuts: Left Arrow (Keep All) / Right Arrow (Sell All).


4. TANK CAPACITY & EXPANSION
----------------------------
- Your starter tank holds up to 50 shrimp.
- If the tank is full, you cannot keep new babies until you sell older shrimp
  or upgrade your tank in the Shop.
- Unlock the "Favorites Tank" in the Shop to create a secondary breeding tank
  holding up to 100 prized shrimp when fully upgraded. Transfer shrimp by clicking the star (★)
  on their profile.


5. EARNING MONEY
----------------
- Sell adult shrimp: Older and pregnant shrimp sell for bonus money.
- Culling: Sell unwanted shrimplets to earn a bit of money.
- Food Preparation: Click "Prep Shrimp Food" in the bottom control panel
  to play cooking minigames and earn up to $30 per session.
- Unlock Arcades: Purchase the Evolution Console or Clado Scanner in the
  Shop to unlock bonus arcade minigames.
- Daily Income: You receive daily income for every 24 in-game hours passed.


6. SAVING YOUR PROGRESS & IMPORTANT WARNINGS
--------------------------------------------
- The game automatically saves when you make purchases or cull babies,
  as well as when you press the "Save" or "Save & Exit" buttons.

- [WARNING] ALWAYS SAVE MANUALLY BEFORE CLOSING:
  Closing the tab or browser directly will NOT automatically save
  unsaved progress. Always click the "Save" or "Save & Exit" button
  in the top bar before leaving the game!

- [WARNING] CLEARING BROWSER CACHE / SITE DATA:
  Your aquarium progress is saved inside your browser's local storage.
  If you clear your browser cache, cookies, or website data, YOUR
  SAVE FILE WILL BE PERMANENTLY ERASED.

- [WARNING] INCOGNITO / PRIVATE BROWSING:
  Never play long-term sessions in Private/Incognito mode, as the
  browser will automatically wipe all data when the window closes.

7. PLANT UPGRADES & PASSIVE BONUSES
-----------------------------------
- Christmas Moss: Males can breed up to 2 females per check.
- Amazon Frogbit: Reduces female resting time by 15%.
- Java Moss: Reduces pregnancy duration by 15%.
- Water Sprite: Increases the number of babies born per clutch by 15%.
- Hornwort: Shrimp mature 15% faster.
- Mutation Algae & Marimo Balls: Boost genetic mutation chances.


8. SPECIES SPECIAL ABILITIES
----------------------------
- Bamboo Shrimp: Generates passive income ($2/min per live adult).
- Legendary Scud: Speeds up maturation by 10% for all shrimp in the tank.
- Red Crawfish: Increases sale values of other shrimp in the tank by +10%.
- Blue Crawfish: Reduces pregnancy duration by 15%.
- Rudolph Red Nose: Reduces female rest time by 20%.
- Vampire Shrimp: Increases tank capacity by +5 per shrimp.
- Galaxy Sulawesi: Increases mutation rate by +5%.
